## `imprint-project-service_project-build`

Build a target and record it: stale figure steps first (`shell` steps only with `allow_shell`), then the document engine — Typst from memory, Markdown through Typst, LaTeX through a materialised directory (`tectonic` embedded, or `pdflatex`/`xelatex`/`lualatex`/ `latexmk` from the system). A `manuscript-build@1.0.0` row holds the outcome, the PDF goes to the workspace CAS, and what steps produced becomes `output` rows derived from their source. `entry_override` is the live buffer.

- **safety**: `external`
- **reads**: "manuscript", "manuscript-file@1.0.0", "figure", "task@1.0.0", "task-event@1.0.0"
